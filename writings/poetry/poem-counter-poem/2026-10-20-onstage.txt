Onstage
<subtitle>A dragging-over and -through from the original Greek of Giorgos Sepheris.<\subtitle>

|**I**
Sun, you play with me
and yet, this is no dance
this such nudity
blood nearly
barbarous home-
less:
then—

|**II**
At the semaphore sound
they came, the pure,
the journeymen far,
bearers of mandates with 
offerings white of towels
heaping and fresh. Their
lilt and lall were lost
to me.
I did not expect them.
I awed. I stammered,
“I love the spectacle.”
Immediately the mussel was peopled with eyes 
fixed to its ribbing, I turned then to the dank parquet 
where there came down that moment a light
to reveal a scene set for some famous murder.

|**III**
And you, what did you seek? Barely arisen
your aspect still heavy with stammer
the linens forsaken and cold
the baths, they were cold and vengeful
your hair was heavy and wet dripping
in droplets bare down your back that glistened
in pools round your navel exposed 
to the dew on the long
of your legs and your feet
so nude to the grass
and the dark earth.
They, three
personae of the lion-muzzler came.
They gyred for you
your eyes like two tragic urchins
like two tumid stones those dark
cherries, your breasts.
(Was this the spectacle’s artifice, I cannot say)
They howled and they tore in the howling the wind.
You, rooted, remained.
The daggers whirred in the drawing the 
colonnade burst with their shrieks.
And you, rooted, remained,
cypress.
They clutched and they clambered they sought
a spot where to strike
when at last, you spoke,
“This handful of rain is yours for the taking
for I am the sea.”

|**IV**
The sea: what is become of the sea?
I lingered too long on overgrown slopes
I was blinded by light-bugs.
And now at this tip of the ocean
I expect for a man
for a wrack or a skiff.

But does the sea welt?
In the twilight white, the fin of a dolphin
tore her ethereal web. Or again,
the tip of the wing of a gull.

And yet her sway was sweet when,
a child, I let myself swell in her plunge,
or again, a bold young man,
searching for patterns in sea-stones
schemas and rhythms.
And the old beard
with bastel and wand
he came and he said unto me
“I am your topos and tropos.
It fares that perhaps I am no one.
Perhaps, it fares that I am whatever you want.”

|**V**
What sort were they that heard at midday
the whirring of daggers?
What sort that came cavalier
with the kindling and the torches?
They washed their hands
of it.
Who unfastened
the woman the infant and the house?
Implicate none. Rack.
The hooves they beat in the fleeing.
They gouged their martyrdom out with their eyes
witness to no thing.

|**VI**
When will you speak again?
Our words are not our own, are
the children of many and lost.
Like that pine tree that stands on a cliff by the sea
holds in its warped silhouette
the shape of the wind
after the wind has crashed out,
so too our words the shape of the baffled dead.
What words did they speak on that night
When the stars weightlessly trampled your
frame, such nudity,
the swan, the archer, the scorpion?
What did they require?
And where will you be when
once again onto the glade there 
comes down for a moment a light?


|**VII**
And yet, under the black-lipped gaze
of the cave on the further shore,
suns in your eyes and rooks on your wings,
you were. You suffered
the other burden, you loved,
the other dawn, you were there,
the other birth, you rose up again.
There you were becoming anew,
in the overfull instant of time,
moment by moment, a drip of sap,
the stalactite, the stalagmite.
